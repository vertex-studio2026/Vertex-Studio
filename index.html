FAQ

Straight answers before you commit.

Still unsure about something? Email pranay.gd266@gmail.com and ask directly.

Are the projects above real client work?+

No. They are self-initiated concept samples and are labelled that way. Vertex Studio will only publish a client name, quote, or result when there is permission and the claim can be supported.

How accurate are the pricing ranges?+

They are useful starting ranges for common scopes. Your price depends on content readiness, page count, functionality, and timeline. The approved proposal is the price used for the agreed scope.

How are payments handled?+

Projects are usually split into two or three documented milestones. The payment schedule is included in the proposal, so you know what is due and which deliverable it relates to.

Who owns the finished website?+

After final payment, you receive the agreed final files and access credentials. Any third-party licences that cannot be transferred are identified before purchase.

Do you guarantee sales or search rankings?+

No. A website can create a strong foundation, but no responsible studio can guarantee a specific sales or ranking result. The work will focus on usability, clear messaging, sound technical basics, and measurable next steps.

Can you improve an existing website?+

Yes, when the current platform and codebase are suitable. Share the site link first; the proposal will say whether an update or a clean rebuild is the more sensible route.
